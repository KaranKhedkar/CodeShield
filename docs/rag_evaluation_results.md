## 5. Mathematical Formulations & Interview Talking Points

### Hit Rate @ $K$
$$\text{Hit Rate}@K = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{I}(\text{target} \in \text{Top-}K_i)$$
Measures the proportion of static analysis queries where the ground-truth CWE or OWASP guidance was included in the retrieved context.

### Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)
$$\text{MRR} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{1}{\text{rank}_i}$$
Measures retrieval ranking precision: rank 1 yields $1.0$, rank 2 yields $0.5$, rank 3 yields $0.33$.

### Functional Re-Scan Pass@1
$$\text{Pass@1} = \frac{\text{Findings with Semgrep Alert Resolved}}{\text{Total Active Findings}}$$
Unlike subjective LLM-as-a-judge scores, Pass@1 is an objective engineering verification: the remediated patch is executed through Semgrep CLI to ensure the security rule no longer triggers.
